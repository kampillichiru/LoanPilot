# LoanPilot Bank: Retail Unsecured Personal Loan Credit Policy

Document id: credit-policy-v1
Effective from: 1 April 2026
Owner: Retail Credit Risk

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT for the LoanPilot learning project. LoanPilot Bank is fictional. These rules are
> invented for testing and are not RBI regulations or any real bank's policy.

## 1. Purpose and scope

### §1.1 Purpose
This policy sets the minimum credit standards for unsecured personal loans to individual customers. Credit
officers, the automated decision engine and the credit memo must all apply the same rules.

### §1.2 Products covered
The policy covers unsecured personal loans for any personal purpose, such as medical expenses, education,
travel, weddings and home renovation. It does not cover home loans, vehicle loans, gold loans, credit cards,
overdrafts or business loans, which have their own policies.

### §1.3 Decision authority
Eligibility, limits and the approve or reject recommendation are produced by the bank's rules engine
using the clauses in this policy. A credit officer reviews every recommendation and takes the final
decision. Assistants and chat tools may explain this policy but must not approve or reject an application.

## 2. Definitions

### §2.1 Net monthly income
Net monthly income is the take-home salary credited to the applicant's bank account after tax, provident
fund and other statutory deductions. For salaried applicants it is the average net pay of the last three
salary slips. Bonuses, incentives and reimbursements are excluded unless paid every month.

### §2.2 Existing obligations
Existing obligations are all monthly EMIs the applicant currently pays on loans and credit cards as shown in
the credit bureau report. For credit cards, 5% of the outstanding balance is counted as a monthly
obligation.

### §2.3 FOIR
FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) is the total of existing obligations plus the EMI of the proposed
loan, divided by net monthly income, expressed as a percentage.
Example: existing EMIs of 20,000 rupees plus a proposed EMI of 16,607 rupees on a net monthly income of
85,000 rupees gives a FOIR of 43.1%.

### §2.4 Bounce
A bounce is an EMI, cheque or auto-debit that was returned unpaid by the applicant's bank, as seen in the
bank statement or the bureau report.

## 3. Applicant eligibility

### §3.1 Age
The applicant must be at least 21 years old when applying and not older than 60 years when the loan ends.

### §3.2 Employment type
Salaried employees of private companies, public sector companies and government bodies are eligible.
Self-employed professionals (doctors, chartered accountants, architects) are eligible under the
self-employed limits in this policy. Other self-employed applicants are not covered by this policy.

### §3.3 Minimum net monthly income
The minimum net monthly income is 25,000 rupees for salaried applicants and 40,000 rupees for
self-employed professionals.

### §3.4 Employment stability
Salaried applicants need at least 12 months of total work experience and at least 6 months with the
current employer. Self-employed professionals need at least 3 years in practice.

### §3.5 Residency
The applicant must be an Indian resident with a current address in a city where the bank has a branch.

## 4. Credit and affordability rules

### §4.1 Affordability principle
The bank lends only what the applicant can repay from regular income without hardship. Affordability is
measured with FOIR (§2.3) and capped by the loan amount limits below.

### §4.2 Maximum FOIR for salaried applicants
For salaried applicants, FOIR must not exceed 50% of net monthly income, including the EMI of the proposed
loan. An application above 50% is not eligible unless the amount is reduced or the tenure extended until
FOIR is 50% or lower.

### §4.3 Maximum FOIR for self-employed professionals
For self-employed professionals, FOIR must not exceed 45%, because their income varies more from month to
month than a salary does.

### §4.4 Applicants with no credit history
An applicant with no credit history (no CIBIL score) may be considered only if net monthly income is at
least 50,000 rupees, and the loan amount is capped at 3 lakh rupees.

### §4.5 Minimum CIBIL score
The applicant's CIBIL score must be 700 or higher. An applicant with a score below 700 is not eligible.
Applicants scoring 750 or higher are eligible for the preferential interest rate in §6.2.

### §4.6 Recent credit enquiries
More than 4 credit enquiries in the last 6 months indicates credit hunger. Such applications must be
referred to a credit officer for manual review before any offer is made.

### §4.7 Maximum loan amount
The maximum loan amount is 20 times net monthly income for salaried applicants and 15 times net monthly
income for self-employed professionals, subject to an absolute cap of 25 lakh rupees for any applicant.

### §4.8 Bounces and payment history
The applicant must have no bounces in the last 6 months. Any account 90 or more days past due in the last
24 months makes the applicant not eligible.

## 5. Loan terms

### §5.1 Loan amount range
The minimum loan amount is 50,000 rupees and the maximum is 25 lakh rupees.

### §5.2 Tenure
The tenure is between 12 and 60 months. The loan must end before the applicant turns 60 (§3.1).

### §5.3 Prepayment
Part-prepayment is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid. A full foreclosure charge of 4% of the outstanding
principal applies in the first 12 months and 2% after that.

## 6. Pricing

### §6.1 Standard interest rate
The standard interest rate is 12% per annum on a reducing balance.

### §6.2 Preferential interest rate
Applicants with a CIBIL score of 750 or higher and a FOIR of 40% or lower get a preferential rate of 11% per
annum.

### §6.3 Processing fee
The processing fee is 1.5% of the loan amount, with a minimum of 2,000 rupees, plus GST.

## 7. Documents

### §7.1 Salaried applicants
Salaried applicants must submit the last 3 salary slips, 6 months of bank statements showing salary credits,
PAN and Aadhaar for KYC, and the latest Form 16.

### §7.2 Self-employed professionals
Self-employed professionals must submit 2 years of income tax returns with computation of income, 12 months
of bank statements, a professional qualification certificate, and PAN and Aadhaar for KYC.

### §7.3 Verification
Salary on the slips must match the salary credits in the bank statement within 5%. The applicant's name
must match across the salary slip, bank statement and KYC documents.

## 8. Exceptions

### §8.1 Deviations
A credit officer may approve a deviation from §4.2, §4.5 or §4.7 only with written justification and
approval from the regional credit head. Deviations from §3.1, §4.8 or §5.1 are not allowed.
